Privacy Policy for PerimeterIO

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

PerimeterIO ("the App") is a wireless and local network reconnaissance utility developed and published by AB Development Labs LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and designed PerimeterIO with an offline-first architecture. We do not operate external backend servers, we do not require user accounts, and we do not collect, sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

All scanning, mapping, and network analysis performed by PerimeterIO happens directly on your Android device. Depending on the features you use, the App accesses and processes the following data locally:

  • Precise Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Android requires location permission to scan nearby Wi-Fi networks and capture GPS coordinates. PerimeterIO uses your device's GPS coordinates solely to plot scanned Wi-Fi access points onto the in-app map.
  • Wi-Fi & Network State (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, INTERNET): Used to scan nearby wireless networks (SSID, BSSID, frequency band, signal strength, security protocol), connect to selected networks, and probe user-specified local IP subnets and ports.
  • User-Initiated Exports: When you choose to export scan results (.txt reports), the App writes that file directly to the local storage location you select via Android's system file picker.

Data Retention: PerimeterIO does not transmit your scan results, IP addresses, or GPS pins to AB Development Labs LLC. Any saved records or exported files remain exclusively on your device under your control until you clear the App's data, delete the exported files, or uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services (Google Maps SDK)

PerimeterIO integrates the Google Maps SDK for Android to display recorded Wi-Fi GPS pins on an interactive map. When the map view is loaded, Google Maps may collect diagnostic information, device identifiers, and map interaction telemetry directly from your device in accordance with Google’s privacy practices.

For more details, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

PerimeterIO is a technical network utility intended for general audiences and IT/security practitioners. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security

Because PerimeterIO stores and processes all scan data locally on your device without transmitting it to our servers, the security of your exported reports and saved scan data depends on your device's built-in security controls (such as screen lock and device encryption).

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in app functionality or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
